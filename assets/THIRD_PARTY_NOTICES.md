# Idle Hands artwork provenance

This notice covers the artwork shipped with Idle Hands. The project owner has
confirmed that all artwork was created specifically for the game, either
procedurally or with AI generation. No artwork was obtained under a third-party
asset licence, so no third-party art attribution or licence notice is required.

| Asset | Provenance recorded in Git | Licence status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/art/cabinet_texture.png` | Generated specifically for Idle Hands and first committed by Kalaith on 19 August 2026 in commit `6374cfbc18599a655cd4c10502e2caa421bf5634`. | Project artwork; no third-party asset licence or attribution required. |
| `assets/art/frogger_frog.png` | Generated specifically for Idle Hands and first committed by Kalaith with the Frogger artwork on 22 August 2026 in commit `d9744f0557586a62bf79cd24975dafd5866d1315`; replaced with a forward-facing generated version in commit `4bfae318bc01939b714ac8af4a76211b536e417b`. | Project artwork; no third-party asset licence or attribution required. |
| `assets/art/frogger_car.png` | Generated specifically for Idle Hands and first committed by Kalaith with the Frogger artwork on 22 August 2026 in commit `d9744f0557586a62bf79cd24975dafd5866d1315`. | Project artwork; no third-party asset licence or attribution required. |

The remaining visual elements are drawn procedurally by the game at runtime and
do not depend on separately licensed art assets. This provenance statement
records the project owner's confirmation alongside the available Git history.

Idle Hands also uses Rust, macroquad, and macroquad-toolkit. Their dependency
licences are governed by their respective distributions.
